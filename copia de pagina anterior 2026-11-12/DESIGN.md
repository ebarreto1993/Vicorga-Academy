# Vicorga Academy Design System

## Direction

Commercial beauty academy with an editorial, premium character. The approved reference is the Photoshop draft supplied by the client: ivory and white sections, deep black brand bands, warm gold actions, large aspirational photography and high-contrast serif headlines.

## Typography

- Display: Playfair Display, weight 500, including selective italic emphasis.
- Interface and body: DM Sans, weights 400–700.
- Eyebrows may use compact tracked uppercase as in the client reference.
- Headlines should remain short, elegant and high contrast.

## Colour

- Paper: `#fbf8f3`
- Cream: `#f1ebe2`
- Ink: `#11110f`
- Gold: `#b9782f`
- Light gold: `#dfa95f`
- WhatsApp: `#20b85b`

Gold is reserved for actions, labels and small accents. Black sections provide contrast. Photography carries the beauty category.

## Layout

- Shared content edge: maximum 1180px with 24px desktop and 16px phone gutters.
- Homepage hero: text and proof on the left, dominant portrait on the right.
- Careers: compact photographic grid on desktop and two columns on phones.
- Brand proof: dark split section with a large practice photograph and three light benefit cards.
- Career detail hero: dark photographic field followed by presentation video and admissions form.
- Career modules: dark horizontal rows using the real dynamic curriculum data.

## Components and motion

- Small-radius rectangular buttons and cards; avoid pill-shaped containers except the career badge.
- Linked photography lifts 2px and zooms no more than 1.03 on precise pointer devices.
- Buttons press to 0.97 scale.
- No automatic carousel, parallax or decorative looping animation.
- Mobile keeps the WhatsApp action reachable and respects reduced motion.

## Content rules

- Never invent student counts, placement percentages, certifications or prices.
- Use the real graduate photographs for testimonials and hero imagery where appropriate.
- Course title, duration, modules, investment, inclusions and schedules remain driven by `datos-carreras.js`.
